## Exam sheet conventions (from official sample)
The sample opens with the notice "AFTER EACH PART OF THE TEST YOU WILL FIND AN ANSWER KEY. THE LENGTH OF THE ANSWERS BELOW IS TYPICAL IN THE B.U. PROFICIENCY TEST. IT IS ADVISED THAT YOU KEEP YOUR ANSWERS SHORT. ANY ADDITIONAL INFORMATION IN YOUR ANSWERS WILL BE TO YOUR DISADVANTAGE." Answer sheets require initials only (name hidden) — matches YADYOK blind-grading policy.

## 1. Selective Listening ("Family Life" — 9 questions)
Examiner script: "Now you will hear a lecture. While you are listening to the lecture, you must answer the questions. The questions are in the order the information is delivered in the lecture. Write only short answers. Complete sentences are not necessary. Before the lecture begins, you will have three minutes in which to study the questions. At the end of the lecture you will be given three minutes to go over what you have written."
Characteristics observed:
- All items are wh- short-answer fill-ins (some sentence-completion with blanks, e.g. "What differentiates ... is _____ of children")
- One item asks for ONE factor from the lecture (accepting multiple alternatives, key shows "peer pressure / parents' level of education")
- Answer key: 2-5 word short phrases; "Write only short answers" rule repeated on question sheet
- 9 items, same order as lecture information flow

## 2. Careful Listening for Main Ideas ("Expression of Emotions" — 10 questions)
Examiner script: "For the second lecture you will have to take notes while you are listening. Do not attempt to write everything down. Just note down the main points and examples. Do not look at the questions before the lecture is over. At the end of the lecture you will have 15 minutes to answer the questions using your notes."
Characteristics observed:
- Questions come AFTER listening; 15-minute answer window
- Mix of: definition recall ("What is the exact definition of..."), note-based short answers, one MULTIPLE CHOICE case item (A-F options, choose letter), one "write one" factor item, fill-in-the-blank sentence
- Answer key again 2-4 word phrases

## 3. Reading I (45 minutes) — "Zheng He: China's Skilled Admiral"
Instructions: "Answer the questions in the spaces provided. Give SHORT and PRECISE answers. For multiple choice questions, write the letter of the correct answer in the blank provided for each question. You have 45 minutes to complete this part. At the end of 45 minutes, this booklet will be collected."
Article: 13 numbered paragraphs, ~950 words, titled academic article with section headings ("The Treasure Voyages", "Aftermath and Controversies", "Destruction of Records").
Question types observed (11 items):
1. Vocabulary-in-context MC (4 options) — "upheaval"
2. Paragraph purpose MC (4 options)
3. Short-answer sentence completion — "The Chinese valued the gifts... because it confirmed that ___"
4. Meaning-in-context MC — "soft power"
5. Main idea of paragraph MC
6. Short-answer causal — "due to ___"
7. NOT-mentioned MC (long option list, choose letter)
8. Paragraph relationship MC — "How does paragraph 12 relate to paragraph 11?"
9. Inserted-sentence task — missing sentence placed at A/B/C/D in paragraph
10. Cross-text comparison MC (given a short second passage about Columbus)
11. Inference MC — "What CANNOT be inferred from the whole text?"
→ Reading I = mostly short-answer + MC; answer key shows letters or short phrases.

## 4. Reading II (50 minutes) — "Cross-Cultural Adaptation"
Article: ~10+ numbered paragraphs academic prose with in-text citations (Anderson 1994, Taft 1973...). (Truncated in extraction; continues with Reading II questions, likely similar mix incl. matching.)

## Key structural takeaways for AI mock generation
1. Selective Listening questions = ONLY wh- short-answer sentence completions (NO multiple choice in this official sample); answers 2-5 words.
2. Careful Listening = mixed: definitions, factor/cause short answers, occasional MC case items (A-F), fill-in-the-blank.
3. Reading = short-answer sentence completions + 4-5 option MCs (vocabulary-in-context, paragraph purpose, main idea, NOT-mentioned, inserted sentence at A/B/C/D, paragraph relation, cross-text, inference).
4. Every part has an ANSWER KEY after it (official samples include keys).
5. Strict "short and precise" answers; extra info penalized.
6. Timings: Listening pre-read 3 min + check 3 min; Careful answer 15 min; Reading I 45 min, Reading II 50 min; Writing 2 essays x 40 min (1h20 total).

## 5. Reading II (50 minutes) — "Cross-Cultural Adaptation"
Question types observed:
- MC with 4 options (letter answers, blanks on the right of the options)
- Short-answer open questions tied to specific cited studies ("According to the results of... write short answers")
- MATCHING task: 3 short supporting texts matched to paragraph numbers (5, 6, 8, 11) with one extra/unused paragraph number → classic BUSEPT paragraph-matching
- Answer key shows letters (A-D), one-word answers ("Marginality", "separation"), and paragraph numbers ("Paragraph 8", "Paragraph 6", "Paragraph 11")

## 6. Test of Writing (1h20)
Official instructions: "You will have to write 2 essays in English. Try to show that your written English is good enough for university study. (Do not, for example, use only very short, simple sentences.) Write neatly in ink. The test lasts 1 hour 20 minutes. You are advised to spend 40 minutes on each essay. This booklet consists of 4 pages."
TASK 1: "On the opposite page, write an essay discussing the negative effects of globalization. The following points are given as guidelines. You may use these or any other points you wish to use." → 2 argumentative/discussion essays with helper-idea guidelines.
